Our Mission

Building More Than Homes — Building Futures

At Affordable Horizon Housing Foundation, we believe stable housing is the foundation of everything else — health, education, and economic mobility. We work hand-in-hand with families, local governments, and community partners to close the affordability gap and create lasting change.



Founded on the principle that no family should choose between paying rent and putting food on the table, we provide housing assistance, financial counseling, tenant advocacy, and new construction support in underserved communities.

"Housing is not just a roof. It is a place where children do their homework, where parents plan for tomorrow, where communities take root and grow."
— AHHF Mission Statement

What We Do

Programs That Move People Forward

Six core programs addressing the full spectrum of housing need — from prevention to ownership.

🏠

Emergency Housing Assistance

Rapid-response rental support for families facing eviction, utility shutoffs, or sudden housing instability due to job loss, illness, or disaster.

Apply Now →
🌱

Affordable New Construction

We develop and co-develop below-market-rate housing units in partnership with city agencies, ensuring affordability for 30+ years.

Learn More →
🤝

Homeownership Pathways

Down payment assistance, first-time buyer education, and credit counseling to help renters transition to homeownership and build generational wealth.

Learn More →
⚖️

Tenant Rights & Advocacy

Free legal guidance, mediation services, and policy advocacy to protect tenants from unjust evictions, discrimination, and unsafe conditions.

Learn More →
📚

Financial Literacy

Workshops, one-on-one coaching, and online tools to help families budget, save, build credit, and achieve long-term housing stability.

Learn More →
♻️

Green & Sustainable Housing

Retrofitting older affordable units with energy-efficient upgrades to lower utility costs for low-income families and reduce environmental impact.
